# Low Battery

You fell asleep on the last bus and woke up locked in the depot. The power is out. Your phone is your only light, and it runs on your **real battery**.

- Lower real battery = dimmer, flickering flashlight.
- Plug your phone in and the light steadies... but something in the dark can hear the charger.
- Two modes: Original (just you and the Dimmer) or Zombies (zombies drawn to your light, plus a gun to fight back).
- Find 3 fuses, power the gate, get out. 3 endings.
- If your browser won't share the battery level, you get an in-game battery that drains.

Play:
- Browser: https://buggygamesyt.github.io/lowbattery/
- Android: grab `LowBattery.apk` from the latest release (reads your real battery on any Android, GrapheneOS included).

Controls: WASD / arrows move, mouse aims, Space hide, click or E shoot (Zombies mode), F light on/off, P pause, M mute. Touch: drag left side to move, drag right side to aim, HIDE, LIGHT and SHOOT buttons.
